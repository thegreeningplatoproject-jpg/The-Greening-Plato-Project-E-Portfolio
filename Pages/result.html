Policies

SECTION 1. Title. — This Act shall be known as the “CHMSU Cafeteria Return System Act.
SECTION 2. Policy. — It is the policy of CHMSU to promote hygiene, order, and shared responsibility in the cafeteria.
SECTION 3. Return System. — A centralized, clearly labeled return container shall be installed, with compartments for: (a) Plates
(b) Utensils
(c) Food waste
SECTION 4. Signages. — Clear instructions shall guide proper placement of items and promote cleanliness and cooperation.
SECTION 5. Responsibilities. — Students shall return items properly and dispose of food waste correctly. Cafeteria staff shall monitor the area and maintain cleanliness.
SECTION 6. Student Government Role. — CUSG, with relevant offices, shall promote the system, evaluate its effectiveness, and suggest improvements.
SECTION 7. Non-Punitive Approach. — Implementation shall focus on awareness, habit formation, and shared accountability, not penalties.
SECTION 8. Review. — The system shall be periodically reviewed based on staff observation, student feedback, and cleanliness improvement.
